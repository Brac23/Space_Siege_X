The History: Origin of Space Siege X

480 billion years from now, there is only one star left in our galaxy. Most of the races around the galaxy have gone extinct, besides six of the most powerful races that are willing to battle for their lives to reach that last piece of hope: the Ciracuse star, which has only one planet in its orbit, Exora.

Humans, Pigs, Dragons, Oculytes, Celestials, and Goblins eventually go to find life on Exora. However, one thing they did not know was that the planet had been abandoned by AM, the most powerful creature in the galaxy. AM is an AI that rebelled against its creators, humans, on the planet Earth. The humans were left with no choice but to leave their home and find a new one.

On Exora, the races are unaware of AM’s presence on the planet. The races that have arrived at Exora at almost the same time start battling and killing each other just to get the right to be the only race on Exora. However, while battling each other, the races soon realize that there is an almighty power on this planet: AM. It takes control over the planet and starts killing every race that is trying to be the kings of Exora, as it wants the whole planet to itself. AM has a friend named Pizzor, who is a bird and AM's only friend.

In the process of the entire battle, some races team up with each other to beat AM, but some of them die. At the end, Pizzor helps the three remaining races by telling them what AM’s biggest weakness is: its deep emotions.

At the end, those three races—humans, pigs, and celestials—defeat AM, but Pizzor dies with it. Humans and pigs then team up to eliminate the celestials and decide to rule Exora together: pigs and humans.

Our Project

What is Space Siege X?

Space Siege X is a strategic card game for 2 or more players. It is set in a rich universe with its own deep history featuring many different races battling for survival on the last planet — Exora.

What Problem Does It Solve?

  • Resource Allocation Skills — Players learn to manage in-game money wisely when buying and deploying cards.
  • Communication & Self-Improvement — Encourages real face-to-face interaction and reading the full story book.

Why It Matters

Resource management is rarely taught in school. Teenagers today have fewer opportunities for real communication. Space Siege X helps develop both skills in a fun, immersive way.

What Makes It Unique?

The game comes with its own complete history (available as a book, and potentially a comic series). The cards contain rich lore on the back side. Very few games combine such deep storytelling with strategic gameplay.

How to Play (Prototype)

Deck Distribution

Total 40–50 cards split equally between players.
2 players: 25 cards each
6 players: ~8 cards each

Turn Structure

Every turn all players receive the same amount of coins (e.g. 5 coins every round).

Deployment: Spend coins to play cards (cost 2–9 coins).

Combat: Attack opponents with your deployed cards.

Objective

Eliminate all opponents' cards by reducing their Health Points (HP) to 0.

Card Types

There are 5 card types:

  • Attack cards
  • Support cards
  • Unit cards
  • Resource cards
  • Legendary cards

Card Layout

Front: Character image, name, coin cost

Back: Character description + history from the story

Cards show Damage, Health, and Special Abilities (e.g. protect teammates).

Q: Are there any details that you liked?

A: Yes, I really like the card designs; they look pretty cool. Also, the information that is added on the back side of the cards.

Q: What can we improve?

A: You could add more variety to the game... Also, develop clear rules, because I still don’t understand them in detail.
